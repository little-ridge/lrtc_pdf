PART E – DISCLOSURE/CONSENT

NBCC is committed to providing an accessible learning environment for all students. If you have any questions or concerns about accessibility or possible accommodations, please contact us as soon as possible (no later than at least one week prior to class start) to discuss.

At the New Brunswick Community College (NBCC), the confidentiality of all information regarding students is treated in accordance with the Right to Information and Protection of Privacy Act (RTIPPA) and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where not already permitted or required by law or legal processes.

NBCC provides student information to the following stakeholders upon request: Student Government, Department of Post-Secondary Education, Training and Labour (including Training Skills Development, Training and Employment Support Services, and Apprenticeship), Education and Early Childhood Development, Student Aid (Student Loans), Statistics Canada, University of New Brunswick Security, and the NBCC Alumni Association.

Graduates will be contacted one year following graduation to participate in the Graduate Follow-Up Survey (if applicable). The Alumni Association will also contact graduates accordingly.

Important notices will be sent to the email account you provided upon registration/application. It is your responsibility to check emails regularly. Changes to your personal information (including email) can be done by signing into Student Self-Service or by contacting nbcc@nbcc.ca.

If any other parties (other than those referenced above) require access to your personal, financial and/ or academic information the student will provide written consent before NBCC can release the requested information.
